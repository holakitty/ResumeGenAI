Single-Column ATS Compliance & System Architecture

Why multi-column resumes fail in enterprise talent screening and how ResumeCraft delivers 100% extraction fidelity.

1 Linear Single-Column Hierarchy

Enterprise parsers (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse) read text linearly top-to-bottom. Two-column or side-by-side tables cause parsers to scramble sentences horizontally. ResumeCraft strictly enforces clean single-column structure with standard H1/H2 headings.

2 Zero Hallucinations & Genuine ISI Records

Preserves all genuine experiences at the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) without fabricating unverified certificates or courses. Reflects actual microdata processing in DBF files, R survey packages, and Advanced Excel.

3 Naukri & Indeed Job Connectors

Presets for real market postings (Fractal Analytics, Ipsos Research, Kantar Insights). Extract technical requirements and keywords, computing real-time ATS match grades with one-click tailoring.
